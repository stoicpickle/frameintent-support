FrameIntent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

FrameIntent is designed to work locally on your device.

No automatic upload or tracking

FrameIntent does not require an account. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app does not upload your projects, reference photos, notes, or exports to a FrameIntent service.

If you voluntarily use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback action, Apple may make your comments, screenshots, device details, or crash information available to the developer. A screenshot may include any project title, note, reference photo, or other content visible when you capture it. You control whether to submit this feedback and whether to include a screenshot.

Data stored on your device

FrameIntent stores project titles, aspect ratios, timestamps, revisions, storyboard notes, frame details, reference photos and thumbnails, integrity details, motion and camera cues, and action notes locally on your device so you can return to and edit your work. This local app data may be included in Apple device backups according to the backup settings you choose.

Camera permission

FrameIntent asks for camera permission only when you choose to capture a reference photo. The camera is used to take that photo for your storyboard project. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

Exports and deletion

FrameIntent does not upload project files automatically. Files leave the app when you choose to share or save an export. Project content can also leave the app if you choose to attach a screenshot containing that content to TestFlight feedback. After you share a copy, the receiving app, service, person, or Apple feedback system handles that copy under its own practices.

Deleting a project removes its local app copy, including its frames, notes, and reference photos. It does not remove copies you have shared or copies retained in Apple device backups. Projects otherwise remain until you delete them or remove the app. FrameIntent has no account or developer-held project data for you to ask the developer to delete.

Support

During an external TestFlight beta, you can use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback action. You can also visit the FrameIntent support page.

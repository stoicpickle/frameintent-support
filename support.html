Support

If you are testing FrameIntent through TestFlight, the fastest support path is TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback action. Please include the step where the problem happened, your iPhone and iOS version, and a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing what is visible.

For a question or issue outside TestFlight, open a public support request. Do not include private photos, storyboard notes, contact details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Open a support request

Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm which frame or project action you were using.
  • Try the action once more after returning to the Projects screen.
  • For camera trouble, check FrameIntent’s permission in iOS Settings.
